Scheme of Study

All 36 courses of the Bachelor of Physiotherapy, year by year, with marks, teaching hours and credits.

The BPT follows an annual pattern: four academic years, each ending in a University examination (BPT I to BPT IV), followed by a one-year rotatory internship. The tables below reproduce the scheme of study in the NCAHP Curriculum Handbook of Physiotherapy (2025). Question papers are set subject-wise. Where RGUHS regulations differ, the University's scheme prevails.

36 courses10 + 9 + 8 + 9 across the four years
6,240 hours1,560 teaching hours in every year
334 credits252 theory + 82 practical
4,250 marksTotal across BPT I to IV

First-year courses

  • BPT-101
    Human AnatomyThe structure of the body, with the musculoskeletal system of the limbs and spine in most detail, plus the thorax, abdomen, head and neck, embryology and neuroanatomy.
  • BPT-102
    Human PhysiologyHow the body works, from cells to organ systems, with emphasis on human movement and how the body responds and adapts to exercise.
  • BPT-103
    BiochemistryMetabolism of carbohydrates, lipids and proteins, enzymes, vitamins, hormones and nutrition, and how exercise changes body chemistry.
  • BPT-104
    Fundamentals of Exercise ModalitiesPrinciples and effects of therapeutic exercise: mechanics, planes and axes, joint measurement, starting positions, transfers, and active, assisted, resisted and passive movements.
  • BPT-105
    Fundamentals of Electro Physical AgentsThe physics of electricity, light and sound, electrical safety, low-frequency currents and TENS, electro-diagnostic tests, superficial heat and the theories of pain.
  • BPT-106
    Psychology & SociologyHuman behaviour, development, motivation and personality, and the social factors that shape health and disability.
  • BPT-107
    Fundamentals of Healthcare Delivery System in IndiaPrimary, secondary and tertiary care, the National Health Mission and Policy, national health programmes and the role of physiotherapy.
  • BPT-108
    English NUESGrammar, speaking and writing (letters, e-mails, reports, case records) and therapeutic communication.
  • BPT-109
    Information Technology NUESComputer basics, office applications, the internet and the role of digital technology in health sciences.
  • BPT-110
    Clinic OrientationVisits across the chain of healthcare delivery, from the Sub-centre, PHC and CHC to district hospitals, medical colleges and private hospitals and clinics.

Second-year courses

  • BPT-201
    Pathology & MicrobiologyCell injury, inflammation, thrombosis and neoplasia, microorganisms and immunity, and infection control in physiotherapy practice.
  • BPT-202
    PharmacologyHow commonly used drugs act, how they affect exercise and movement, and the red and yellow flags for physiotherapy prescription.
  • BPT-203
    Public Health & Health PromotionDeterminants of health, levels of prevention, basic epidemiology, national health programmes and health education.
  • BPT-204
    Emergency Care and Life Support SkillsBasic life support: airway management, CPR, AED use, first aid, triage and safe patient transport, taught on mannequins and through role play.
  • BPT-205
    Exercise TherapyRelaxation, suspension therapy, posture and breathing exercise, stretching, manual therapy and peripheral joint mobilisation, traction, PNF and motor learning.
  • BPT-206
    ElectrotherapyUltrasound, LASER, cryotherapy, TENS, interferential and other currents, shortwave diathermy, biofeedback and hydrotherapy, with dose calculation.
  • BPT-207
    Biomechanics & KinesiologyKinetics and kinematics of human movement, the link between structure and function, and analysis of normal and abnormal posture and gait.
  • BPT-208
    Yoga and Systems of MedicineThe philosophy and history of yoga, asanas, pranayama, relaxation and meditation, and their physiological effects.
  • BPT-209
    Clinical ObservationRotating postings in physiotherapy OPDs and hospital wards, observing patient care and keeping a case portfolio or diary.

Third-year courses

  • BPT-301
    General Medicine and PediatricsCauses, clinical features, diagnosis and management of common medical disorders across the body systems, with a unit on paediatrics.
  • BPT-302
    General SurgeryPrinciples of surgery, wound healing, anaesthesia and post-operative complications, and common surgeries as they affect physiotherapy decisions.
  • BPT-303
    OrthopedicsDisorders and injuries of bones, joints, muscles and soft tissues, special tests, and reading X-rays and scans for physiotherapy practice.
  • BPT-304
    Physiotherapy in Adult and Pediatric Medical and Surgical ConditionsPhysiotherapy for oedema, wounds, burns, abdominal surgery, obstetrics and gynaecology, oncology and palliative care, geriatrics, hypertension and diabetes, and paediatric CBR.
  • BPT-305
    Physiotherapy in Adult and Pediatric Orthopedic ConditionsSOAP-format assessment and evidence-based treatment of fractures, joint disease, spinal conditions, joint replacements, amputations and deformities.
  • BPT-306
    Physical & Functional Diagnosis & PrescriptionDiagnosing impairments, activity limitations and participation restrictions with the ICF framework, using valid assessment tools and special tests.
  • BPT-307
    Research Methodology, Biostatistics and Evidence Based PracticeResearch process and designs, sampling, statistical tests, literature searching and critical appraisal; students prepare a sample research proposal.
  • BPT-308
    Clinical EducationSupervised training in wards and OPDs: bedside approach, assessment, special tests, treatment dosage, outcome measures and case presentations.

Fourth-year courses

  • BPT-401
    Neurology, Psychiatry and NeurosurgeryDisorders of the central and peripheral nervous systems, neuromuscular and psychiatric conditions, and investigations such as CT, MRI, EMG and NCV.
  • BPT-402
    Physiotherapy in Adult and Pediatric Neurological and Neurosurgical ConditionsNeurological assessment and outcome scales, neurophysiological treatment approaches, neurological gait, neurosurgical care and paediatric neurology.
  • BPT-403
    Cardiothoracic Diseases and SurgeriesHeart, blood vessel and respiratory diseases, such as heart failure, ischaemic heart disease, COPD, asthma and TB, and their management.
  • BPT-404
    Physiotherapy in Adult and Pediatric Cardiothoracic Conditions and Surgical ConditionsAirway clearance, lung expansion, breathlessness management, cardiac and pulmonary rehabilitation, ICU physiotherapy and paediatric chest physiotherapy.
  • BPT-405
    Sports Physiotherapy & Exercise PrescriptionInjury prevention, acute and overuse sports injuries, taping and bandaging, on-field emergencies, fitness testing and exercise prescription.
  • BPT-406
    PT Ethics, Medico-Legal Aspects, Management & AdministrationEthics and law, informed consent, confidentiality, negligence and patients' rights, management principles, quality control and running a clinic.
  • BPT-407
    Community Physiotherapy & RehabilitationCommunity-based rehabilitation, disability evaluation, orthotics and assistive devices, vocational rehabilitation, and occupational health and ergonomics.
  • BPT-408
    Project Work Orientation NUESPrepares students for the supervised project (case study, new technique or literature review) submitted during the internship.
  • BPT-409
    Clinical RotationSupervised rotation through ten clinical areas, from the physiotherapy OPD and ICUs to community PHCs, old age homes and industrial visits.

Clinical rotation areas in the fourth year

In Clinical Rotation (BPT-409, 390 hours), students rotate through the areas below and learn, under supervision, bedside approach, patient assessment, special tests, identifying indications, ruling out contraindications, deciding treatment parameters and dosage, and using relevant outcome measures. Evidence-based practice is part of the training.

Physiotherapy OPD
Neurology, Neurosurgery & Neuro ICU
Community – PHC
Orthopedics
General Medicine & MICU
General Surgery & CTS ICU
Developmental Pediatrics & Child Guidance Clinic
OBG
Geriatric – Old Age Homes
Industrial Visits – Ergonomics

Community Physiotherapy & Rehabilitation (BPT-407) practicals add field visits to urban and rural PHCs and regional rehabilitation training centres, regular mobile camps, disability surveys and screening in villages, prescription of ambulatory and assistive devices, making low-cost assistive devices from locally available materials, and community education programmes on health and disability.
